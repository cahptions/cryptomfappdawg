# Council draft: independent design review

Research-only review, 2026-10-19. No runtime or trading behavior changed. These are specification corrections and limitations, not backtest results.

## Decision

Retain exactly two candidates: **A, common-factor-residual continuation**, and **B, temporary execution-dislocation recovery**. A has a clearer immediately computable statistical mechanism once synchronized benchmark history is persisted. B remains experimental and frequently ABSTAIN: stable pool depth plus recovering quote costs does not identify why a price fell or prove that it will revert. Calling B an execution-dislocation hypothesis avoids claiming that a transient liquidity shock has been causally established.

## Candidate A review

The draft uses15m bars,288training return observations (72h),16held-out signal returns (4h), one skipped recent return (15m), and an8h counterfactual maxhold. Parameters are starting hypotheses and need a simpler raw-momentum baseline, not optimization until they look profitable.

1. **Warm-up exceeds72h.** With the decision after return j is available, skip r[j]; use signal r[j−16]…r[j−1]; train r[j−304]…r[j−17] (288observations). This requires305consecutive return intervals and306closed price observations:76h15m between first and last close. First-seen≥72h is an additional eligibility check, not sufficient history. Pool age is separate from token first-seen age. Current200×5m backfill gives only16h40m; accumulate the rest prospectively without stitching pools.
2. Fit token/USD log returns `r_i = alpha + beta * r_SOL + epsilon` using training observations only. Store alpha/beta, train start/end, design-matrix condition/benchmark variance, train residual MAD and all source IDs. A zero/near-zero SOL variance, nonfinite coefficient or zero MAD yields ABSTAIN, not beta=0/1 fallback. Any fallback is a separately registered simple baseline.
3. Define `sigma_MAD = 1.4826 * median(abs(e_train - median(e_train)))`. The held-out signal sum uses the frozen training alpha/beta. `Z = sum(e_signal)/(sigma_MAD*sqrt(16))` is a dimensionless diagnostic. Serial dependence, volatility clustering, coefficient estimation and heavy tails invalidate a normal-tail or p-value interpretation. Any uncertainty/effect assessment uses chronological out-of-sample, jointly blocked evaluation.
4. Raw OLS is outlier-sensitive despite robust residual scaling. Predeclare treatment of bad prices and singular fits; never drop large legitimate losses. Compare coefficient stability on predefined training subwindows and report instability. Clipping/winsorization would be a new registered specification, not an invisible cleaning step.
5. Skipping the latest return can reduce contamination of the signal but must not ignore a current crash, liquidity drain or route disappearance. Current data freshness, newest risk evidence and primary safety controls remain authoritative. A strong old4h residual signal is not permission to chase a move that invalidated in the skipped15m.
6. A residual continuation signal partly overlaps B2/H1/H3/H6. Its claimed additional information is **performance unexplained by lagged SOL exposure**, not a new price indicator. Test raw-momentum-matched returns and residual incremental effects, not just whole-sample correlation. Alpha estimation removes training-period average drift; say explicitly that held-out positive residuals represent new excess continuation relative to that model.
7. A positive residual is not a market-neutral position: long-only token exposure still carries beta risk, crash risk and concentration. Compare the executable SOL-funded long with retained SOL and the unchanged primary account. Do not report a frictionless short-SOL residual hedge as tradeable performance.
8. Parameter sets are asset-specific. The15m/3day/4h/8h configuration is an older-memecoin research specification. Equity sessions/overnight gaps/corporate actions and liquid crypto daily-factor effects require independent specifications and data.

## Candidate B review

The draft needs a negative15m token/SOL shock, stable verified constant-product pool depth, a measured same-ticket round-trip cost spike and recovery, a positive bounce, and sufficient remaining gap. It watches no more than two pre-enrolled pools. Maxhold90m and stop8% are counterfactual rules, not guaranteed loss limits.

1. **Define the shock precisely.** Let q=ln(tokenUSD/SOLUSD). Center the event15m change on the median of96pre-event15m changes; scale by1.4826×their MAD. Require standardized innovation≤−3, positive scale, complete history, and an absolute SOL15m arithmetic return≤1%. Document fixed beta1: a high-beta token can legitimately fall more than SOL without temporary mispricing. The benchmark filter reduces that possibility but does not eliminate it.
2. **Freeze pre-event enrollment and baselines.** A two-pool watcher must already have a valid pre-shock quote-cost and reserve baseline. Discovering a chart shock cannot retroactively create that information. Specify a fixed-ticket baseline estimator, a maximum age, a minimum number of observations, and the exact pre-event window. With absent baseline, ABSTAIN. Do not substitute changing account tickets without repricing every baseline.
3. **Reserve depth is not TVL.** Use decoded same-pool base/quote reserve amounts in unchanged mint units and compute D=sqrt(XY); compare D/D_baseline≥0.95. Check amounts, decimals, slots, mint identities, supported fee/extension rules and pool invariance. This metric does not carry over to concentrated-liquidity, virtual-reserve or dynamic-liquidity pools. Stable invariant depth is evidence against large withdrawal, not proof of executable exit capacity or benign trading.
4. `PoolVerification` currently discards normalized reserve amounts after decoding: `contracts/discovery.py:91` stores USD liquidity/price and raw references; `security/onchain.py:131` parses raw amounts and returns only derived USD fields at:143. Original vault packets can support a validated reconstruction with original reception times, or add prospective normalized reserve evidence. Never substitute `liquidity_usd` for sqrt(XY): it changes with price.
5. **Distinguish pool and route evidence.** The reserve baseline refers to one verified pool; Jupiter may route through other pools or split routes. Either require the preregistered route relationship or explicitly label cross-route evidence and abstain from attributing its cost spike to the verified pool. Unsupported/future/changed pool identity is ABSTAIN.
6. Define positive cost baseline b>0 and observed spike s≥1.5b. Two successive recovery costs must each be≤min(1.2b,0.75s,0.03), measured on independently timed linked quote pairs at least5m apart. The final pair must remain fresh at decision; if stale, another pair rechecks the condition. Do not treat missing route as an infinite measured cost, do not choose the maximum spike retrospectively, and do not confuse a cache refresh/provider outage with liquidity recovery.
7. Define event time as the15m shock bar's end; record first received time separately. Entry expires30m after event end, regardless of delayed reception. A valid sequence could be shock/spike evidence shortly after event end, recovery observations at+5m/+10m, then fresh decision quote; unavailable or late evidence means ABSTAIN. Depth reads must also satisfy explicit freshness at observation and final decision.
8. A quote pair includes sequential SOL→token then exact-output-quantity token→SOL. Shared token/SOL leg, ticket units and network/priority fees are unchanged across baseline/spike/recovery. The3% boundary is the existing quote-implied risk ceiling, not all-in costs. Include modeled failure, latency/MEV/impact deterioration and any unreported fees in the profitability hurdle; avoid double-counting fees already in quote output.
9. Define the pre-event reference and gap causally: for example q immediately before the shock, then current gap `g=q_pre−q_now>0`. Define positive bounce using an observed post-event low followed by a completed higher close and record that chronology; never retrospectively choose the entire future minimum. Remaining half-gap must clear the full cost+margin in consistent units. If the market is trending lower, a bounce plus restored quotes is not evidence that this reference is fair value.
10. The draft2percentage-point margin is a preregistered stress allowance, not an estimated expected edge. A half-gap target and ≤3% costs can demand unusually large still-open dislocations; accept scarce qualifying events rather than relaxing thresholds after observing few trades.
11. A stop8% has no fill guarantee. Evaluate using executable token/SOL exit value; if no route exists, retain an impaired/blocked position and explicit adverse valuation rather than assigning an8% closed loss. Maxhold90m also cannot imply an available exit. Specify intrabar stop/target ambiguity conservatively or use actual quote timestamps;15mOHLC alone cannot order both events.
12. B overlaps H2/H4 and can conflict with H7's exhaustion avoidance. It must not overrule H7. Measure it as separate evidence conditional on those mechanisms, including event overlap, shared quote failures, residual-return correlation and disagreement performance. It is not independent simply because it has a different name.

## Feasibility and role boundaries

- A first version should add0provider requests: reconstruct/persist the existing SOL payload into aligned closed bars and compute report-only features. Current context1h return/volatility cannot supply15m benchmark closes.
- B may be specified immediately but cannot truthfully emit SUPPORT without existing pre-event depth/quote evidence. A later explicit two-pool watcher could budget baseline/recovery probes through shared provider limits, yielding to guardian exits. This research pass does not start such a watcher.
- Under two pools and a single round-trip pair per5m, a minimal baseline schedule alone costs0.8Jupiterrequests/min (2pools×2legs/5min), before spikes, recoveries, rechecks, retries and RPC reserves. That is a schedule calculation, not measured available capacity. Ten PAPER positions can already use roughly24exitquotes/min, leaving materially less headroom than the nominal50/min keyed ceiling suggests.
- A and B use different mechanisms but share token prices, SOL benchmark, security/provider health and quote costs; they are correlated sources of evidence. Neither changes account allocation, primary stops or execution authorization. Scores remain distinct and are never averaged into a pseudo-probability.
- Full three-way experiments must freeze the primary implementation/spec hash; record outcome interval through its actual/maxholding horizon, purge overlapping labels and use calendar blocks spanning all accounts/arms jointly. A's8h and B's90m labels do not shorten H6's24h primary horizon for leakage control.

## Minimum preregistration acceptance

Before collecting any promotion evidence, the machine-readable spec must resolve precise indexing, warm-up, benchmark numeraire, gap/cost units, quote and depth sequence/freshness, pre-enrollment, route comparability, treatment of missing exits, all parameter defaults and baseline definitions. A narrow offline fixture can verify arithmetic and causal availability. Only genuine prospective matched-account outcomes can test incremental net benefit; no performance result is claimed here.
