## Reference: popular options profit calculators

- **OptionStrat** ([features](https://Optionstrat.com/features), [builder tutorial](https://optionstrat.com/tutorials/options-builder)):
  - legs on a strike rail;
  - 50+ strategy templates;
  - stats header: net debit or credit, max loss, max profit, break-evens, chance of profit;
  - P/L table (price × date heatmap) and a line chart;
  - date, IV and range sliders.
- **optionsprofitcalculator.com:** strategy picker, per-leg inputs, a price × date table in $ or %, and a line chart.
- **Robinhood / broker analyzers:** a single P/L line with break-even, max profit and loss, and chance of profit from IV.

## Plan, in order

1. **Shell v2.**
   - Desktop: primary nav of Home, Stocks, Options, Events, Plays, Paper, Learning and Evidence. System and Settings
     become icon buttons, and the worker and latest session show as a compact status pill.
   - Phone: a bottom tab bar (Home, Stocks, Plays, Paper, More); More opens a sheet with the rest. A search icon
     expands a full-width search.
   - Alerts (Schwab sign-in) stay as a banner.
2. **Calculator v2.** A full-screen workspace (`#calc/SYMBOL`, also opened from chains and plays) in OptionStrat
   style:
   - header: symbol, price and the strategy name, detected from the legs;
   - stats strip: net debit or credit, max profit, max loss, break-evens, chance of profit (lognormal with the legs'
     IV) and return on risk;
   - a strike rail with leg pills, and compact leg rows;
   - templates: long call or put, the four verticals, straddle, strangle, iron condor, iron butterfly, butterfly and
     calendar;
   - a Chart or Table toggle. The table is a price × date heatmap in $ or % and is clickable;
   - date, IV and range sliders, plus position Greeks.
   - The modelled versus observed honesty notes stay.
3. **Paper:** fix the strategy picker into a strategy table and widen the page. A real empty state for the chart.
4. **Plays:** a compact filter bar (a Filters drawer on phone), a horizontally scrolling category row, and denser
   cards with the details collapsed.
5. **Home and Options:** a slim collection status, per-row notes moved behind a disclosure, and Options' own hero.
6. **Learning:** a summary hero (equity, P/L, trades) with the tiles behind a disclosure.
7. **Settings, System and Evidence:** step cards, wider text, and no clipped tables.

Each step: screenshots at 1366px and 375px, existing browser workflows kept green, and a commit.
