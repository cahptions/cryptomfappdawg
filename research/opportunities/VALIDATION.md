# Frozen evaluation and promotion contract

This is an experiment design, not a claim that either mechanism has positive expected return. See
[README.md](README.md) for hypotheses and [specifications.json](specifications.json) for the frozen defaults.
The existing two-advisor council is unchanged. These opportunity probes do not add votes to it.

## What would count as earlier, useful information?

For every locally observed opportunity, keep first receipt, source event/bar close, first research WATCH,
primary qualification/decision, first exact-size entry estimate, actual PAPER entry, and the exit timeline.
Define usable lead time as `primary decision - first WATCH` only when an executable entry at the intended
size was actually available then. A positive chart lead with no estimate is **unproven actionability**.
Store missing links and rejection reasons, rather than choosing only completed profitable chains.

The delivered lab measures first receipt, short-window WATCH, as-of primary state and later reference/quote
marks. It does **not** yet join complete entry-to-exit policies, calculate tradable lead time, or estimate
net expectancy. Those are blocked on sufficient observed quotes, quantities, paths and prospective outcomes.
The displayed observation count is not a count of independent trials, and current local coverage is not
the total population of all coins.

## Registered claims and comparisons

Register before collecting the evaluation segment: venue, specification hash, receipt-time eligibility,
capital profile, cohort rule, endpoints, all variants, fee assumptions, maximum label/holding horizon,
missing-exit treatment, cost error reserve and practical economic hurdle. A changed threshold or ticket
creates a new experiment; never reuse the favorable part of an older test as an untouched holdout.
Record failed and abandoned experiments alongside retained candidates.

The two venue hypotheses share a mechanism and are **not independent confirmation**. For each venue compare:

1. Unchanged primary engine, NO TRADE, matched-time BTC, and an exposure-matched equal-weight eligible basket.
2. Price-only control, participation/flow WATCH, and their intersection on the **same observed universe**.
3. Primary plus a WATCH filter; primary plus the original residual-persistence advisor; primary plus the
   original shock/recovery advisor; both original advisors; and primary unchanged. Keep discovery-WATCH
   improvements separate from council improvements to avoid attributing one change to another.
4. An early-entry policy using WATCH, including its additional rejects, failed exits and idle capital.

The current report delivers descriptive group counts, coverage and raw outcomes. It does not execute or
grade these portfolio policies. Counts across ALL/FLOW/PRICE/BOTH overlap and must not be added together.

## Time splits and inference

- Use only observations actually received by each decision. Preserve both event time and receipt time.
  Current Coinbase history fetched today cannot establish an opportunity available yesterday. The lab
  skips old backfilled entries and labels previously received data processed late as REPLAY.
- Allocate chronologically to development, walk-forward evaluation, and an untouched terminal period.
  Fit scalers, liquidity thresholds and any conditional models on training only. Choose window lengths
  from available independent market regimes; do not claim that one day or a fixed trade count suffices.
- Purge any training outcome interval overlapping an evaluation interval. With maximum 24h labels, place
  an embargo of at least 24h plus observed maximum availability delay at adjacent boundaries. For proposed
  6h policies this may be shortened only under a separately registered policy-label scheme. Pool identities
  of the same token belong to the same split and dependence cluster.
- Evaluate paired daily portfolio increments on the same UTC calendar, with initial equity $500, identical
  account-relative exposure and fee/fill models. Keep the $100,000 learning cushion a separate experiment.
  BTC's full-account buy-and-hold, matched-risk BTC and matched-holding-period BTC answer different questions;
  report each with exposure and capital-days. Don't compare a sum of fixed-ticket returns to compounded BTC.
- Use moving/stationary time-block resampling **jointly across assets and policies**. Select block lengths
  on training dependence diagnostics and report sensitivity (including day/week blocks). Cluster same-token
  opportunities and common shocks. Too few effective blocks means insufficient evidence, even with many polls.
- For confirmatory claims use preregistered Holm family-wise correction at 5% over venue, policy and endpoint
  claims. Register sensitivity sweeps as exploratory. Determine power and observation duration from the
  uncertainty in *incremental net return*, not a mandatory magic trade count. Repeatedly peeking at ordinary
  confidence intervals does not create a valid sequential test; use fixed review dates for the first version.
- If the untouched period is inspected or tuning resumes, it is development data. Start a new forward holdout.

## Economic and operational stress

Measure net expectancy, paired incremental dollars, drawdown, expected shortfall, worst unliquidated exposure,
turnover, capital-days, capacity, false-positive attention cost, rejection quality and useful lead time.
Report rank/stance error correlation conditional on primary strategy and market regime. Show leave-one-token,
leave-one-week and leave-top-winner-out results: a tail strategy can depend on a few winners, but cannot hide it.

Use executable BUY debit and original-quantity SELL credit, embedded spread/route fees/impact once, additional
network and failed-attempt fees only where not already included, and measured estimate-to-fill error. Stress
1x/2x/3x observed adverse execution, Wi-Fi/suspend gaps, missing history, venue crossing, delisting, supply
changes, migration and liquidity withdrawal. Those multipliers are **scenarios**, not measured cost quantiles.
Stale prices are never liquidation proceeds. Missing exits remain unresolved exposure; show zero recovery
and explicitly justified recovery bounds. No winsorization that removes the failures being targeted.

Measure the present 100-day/four-frame gate's opportunity cost against a shorter-data **research** cohort.
Distinguish genuinely absent source bars, a bar with real zero volume, source/listing mismatch and late receipt.
A lower volume floor or a compressed history window is a new experimental policy, not a routine bug fix.
Do not manufacture candles to make the current rules eligible. A future no-trade bar representation would
need proof of a complete venue event feed and explicit semantics; a missing REST candle is not that proof.

## Promotion, demotion and removal

First operational gate: correct timestamps, identity, immutable features, reproducible hashes, source/ledger
isolation, bounded work, honest missing outcomes and sufficient executable-size coverage. Until then stay
SHADOW with zero influence. Synthetic tests clear only software gates.

Before inspecting evaluation outcomes choose a minimum worthwhile incremental gain in dollars/capital-days
and a cost-model error reserve. A candidate can enter a separately versioned **PAPER filtering trial** only
when the untouched, multiplicity-adjusted lower bound for paired net improvement exceeds both, downside is
within the registered limits, and results survive missing-exit and execution stresses. Coverage must be high
enough that plausible outcomes of missing observations cannot reverse the conclusion. Also show effect by
regime and sensitivity, not just one aggregate p-value. Failure of these conditions means continue collecting
or reject the hypothesis; do not loosen them after viewing the answer.

Ranking and position sizing require new, separate evaluations after a filter succeeds. No majority vote or
average of unrelated scores may substitute. Never allow a WATCH to relax security, STOP, guardian, liquidity,
fees, account-relative exposure or LIVE authorization. Corrupt/stale inputs, broken identity or safety gates
immediately demote influence to zero. Negative forward evidence at the registered review or cost-stress failure
benches the candidate. If complete coverage yields an upper uncertainty bound below the practical hurdle in
two separately registered evaluation periods, retire it; retain all failed records. These are proposed gates,
not an implemented automatic promoter.

The current Robinhood learning lab's existing PAPER promotion is separate. Its historical tests still have
current-universe survivorship and approximate costs; this research pass does not certify them as live-ready.
