# Research behind the Stocks v1 design

The first question worth a forward test is whether the difference between call and put implied volatility improves selection among stocks KESTREL can already observe. The first question worth an operator test is whether someone looking at a full $1,000 balance can tell what PAPER actually did. Both have observable answers. The studies below inform how to measure them. KESTREL’s forward effect size, attainable fills on the Dell, and operator comprehension are unmeasured here.

## What the implementation measures

| Study | Measured outcome | Decision rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call-minus-put IV | Paired stock price return in basis points, next regular open through fifth close. See the protocol shown in Research for admission and grading deadlines. | The app waits for the 30 scheduled blocks to resolve. At least 24 graded pairs are needed. The one-sided sign test counts a pair as a win when the difference exceeds 10 bp. A final success also requires a mean above 10 bp. |
| Quote allocation | Usable chains, provider operations, elapsed symbol collection time and retained compressed chain bytes during the assigned collection window. Failures remain in the assigned count. | One eligible rotation slot per market session. Cash-limit follow-up and ordinary rotation share the same usability definition. Coverage and cost are descriptive. The follow-up arm also checks the original legs against frozen cash. It requires synchronized later quotes and displayed size. Profitability requires later independent grading. |
| Research transfer | Per-symbol/session pairs of frozen research estimates and fee-inclusive outcomes from closed orders with comparable horizons. | Entry-debit weighting within a group, equal weight across groups. Report sample counts and overlapping exposure. A partial or absent fill remains visible in its own receipt. |

A missed window counts toward the IV study denominator after enrollment. Outcomes may still grade when enrollment is paused. The quote comparison calls an interrupted collection unknown when the completion record never arrives. Both switches are in the Research desk. The simulated engines use the same order policies as before this research release.

At 24 graded observations, an exact one-sided sign test at 5% requires at least 17 wins. Its nominal power is about 56% if the actual independent win chance is 70%, before imposing the separate mean requirement. That power calculation is an assumption check. Shared market shocks, coverage and dependence can make the observed information content lower. Report an underpowered or coverage-limited experiment directly rather than treating it as a losing trading rule.

## Which signal earns a place?

**Empirical asset pricing.** Cremers and Weinbaum compared subsequent stock returns across portfolios formed from the implied-volatility difference between calls and puts with the same strike and expiry. Their working paper weighted pairs by average open interest and used American-option valuations with discrete dividends. The published paper reported a 50-basis-point weekly difference between portfolios of stocks with relatively expensive calls and relatively expensive puts. The effect weakened over their sample. KESTREL uses selected liquid pairs 10–60 calendar days from expiry and a Black–Scholes calculation with trailing dividend yield. Pricing assumptions, selection and the historical period all limit transfer of that estimate. [Deviations from Put-Call Parity and Stock Return Predictability, JFQA 2010](https://doi.org/10.1017/S002210901000013X) · [Authors’ paper, §2.3](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=f3fafcded1695d2b5a07097dd25a3c41c0ad61c3&repid=rep1&type=pdf) · [KESTREL signal calculation](../src/desk/stocks/option_signals.py)

**Proposed decision.** Before opening the next evaluation window, freeze the input cutoff, hypothesis, stock-only comparison, liquidity and risk matching, return horizon, costs, minimum support and one review date. Save the candidates admitted and excluded that day. Compare the two portfolios on the same future sessions, with uncertainty computed across non-overlapping holding periods. Report exclusions and missing outcomes alongside paired returns. A stock-return advantage would justify a separate whole-contract options experiment. The historical research arm can establish feasibility and identify data problems. [Existing historical experiments](../src/desk/stocks/option_research.py)

**Statistical ML challenge.** Gu, Kelly and Xiu reserved separate chronological periods for training, tuning and out-of-sample testing. Their simple equity benchmark used size, value and momentum. Their main variable-importance measure zeroed one predictor while retaining the fitted model. KESTREL already provides a different, useful experiment that *refits* after removing blocks of signals and compares to ridge and seeded random ranks on common candidates. Run `uv run desk stocks learner-ablate --source forward --rows 20000` and retain rows, days, folds, rank correlation and daily top-decile lift. Report an unavailable baseline when support is insufficient. Once someone has used previously recorded forward data to choose a version, its untouched test starts with subsequent decisions. [Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning, RFS 2020](https://dachxiu.chicagobooth.edu/download/ML.pdf) · [Existing ablation](../src/desk/stocks/learner/ablation.py)

**A limit on unusual-activity claims.** Pan and Poteshman had exchange classifications for buyer-initiated opening trades. Schwab’s chain parser receives aggregate contract volume. Johnson and So studied aggregate volume with a different design, sorting weekly on the option-to-stock volume ratio, skipping one session, then measuring the next five sessions of stock returns. Their main option universe used 5–35 trading days to expiry. KESTREL sums the selected expiries through 120 calendar days. Use the term *observed activity* and retain chain coverage if studying this next. A large print alone cannot establish buyer intent. [Pan and Poteshman, RFS 2006](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhj024) · [Johnson and So, JFE 2012](https://eso.scripts.mit.edu/docs/The-option-to-stock-volume.pdf) · [Schwab parser](../src/desk/stocks/sources/schwab.py)

## What would execution and collection change?

**Options market microstructure.** Muravyev and Pearson found that some traders timed execution with respect to subsequent option-price changes. Their appendix compared one-minute quote snapshots with ten-minute midpoint movements, actual trades and displayed size. Their cost estimates belong to those data and timing rules. For KESTREL, use the stored timestamp of each exact leg to compare the decision midpoint, next fresh bid/ask and subsequent fixed-horizon midpoint. Record the side and age of each quote, leg-time differences, visible size, fees, passes without a supported quote and contracts the book could afford. Keep price movement after a decision distinct from queue priority, a partial fill or a simultaneous spread fill, which snapshots alone cannot resolve. Report $1,000 and $5,000 results separately. [Options Trading Costs Are Lower than You Think, RFS 2020](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhaa010) · [Authors’ appendix, Table IA.11](https://www.dmurav.com/MuravyevPearson.OptionsTradingCosts.InternetAppendix.pdf) · [Existing forward comparison](../src/desk/stocks/learner/forward.py)

**Data engineering challenge.** The top-ranked plays receive attention and can accumulate better paths than the candidates they beat. As a collection experiment, pair a fixed fraction of marginal quote requests for selected plays with randomly assigned peers matched on risk, expiry and book affordability. Keep the default collector as a control. Measure extra complete, gradable pairs per 100 requests, collection hour, gigabyte and incremental dollar. Retain requests with no quote in the denominator and report zero-cost feeds in requests and storage. Test a shorter cadence against more names this way before buying history. [Existing quote collection](../src/desk/stocks/play_tracking.py)

## Can someone tell what happened?

**HCI.** Amershi and colleagues had 49 design practitioners inspect 20 AI-enabled products. Their guidelines cover making a system’s capabilities clear, managing uncertainty and explaining the reason for an output. Fernandes and colleagues tested actual choices in an incentivized transit task. A frequency display improved expected decision payoff there. A financial analogue worth testing is plain language showing how many comparable cases matured, how many are missing and which number is a model estimate. A small observed sample should display its actual count. The financial task needs its own evaluation. [Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction, CHI 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf) · [Uncertainty Displays Using Quantile Dotplots or CDFs Improve Transit Decision-Making, CHI 2018](https://www.mjskay.com/papers/chi2018-uncertain-bus-decisions.pdf)

**Proposed operator test.** Counterbalance the old and proposed views using equivalent PAPER fixtures at 390- and 1440-pixel widths. Ask operators to identify the book, explain the full balance from its ledger and latest decision, choose a whole-contract idea within a stated cash budget, and distinguish a measured outcome from an estimate. Record correct answers, completion without help, seconds per task and wrong-book or unsupported-fill conclusions. A proposed first gate is at least 9 correct explanations in 10 trials at each width and zero mistaken claims of a fill or top-up. Record participant counts as well as trials. Measure the old view first. A rendering check can verify visible labels and placement. Only users can establish the task baseline. [Current finite-book policy](../src/desk/stocks/learning_retail.py)

**Reliability engineering challenge.** Pillai and colleagues tested persistence across six Linux filesystems and checked whether failures left an application-consistent result. Apply that method to an interrupted PAPER write on a disposable database. Interrupt before and after an event commit, restart twice, then compare cash movements, position state and order identity with an uninterrupted run. Require SQLite integrity, one event per logical transition, a reconciled balance and a visible worker checkpoint. Measure warm and cold recovery on the Dell with the same workload. A process interruption tests a different fault from physical storage losing power. [On the Complexity of Crafting Crash-Consistent Applications, OSDI 2014](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi14/technical-sessions/presentation/pillai) · [Existing reliability measurements](../research/reliability/HANDOFF.md)

**Building the application over time.** Sculley and colleagues describe hidden feedback loops, data dependencies and undeclared consumers in production ML systems. KESTREL has a specific loop worth testing. A play can get more quotes because the current ranking puts it on screen. That extra coverage can make the same play easier to grade and the ranking look better. Log why each observation was collected and retain a comparison sample from ordinary rotation. Check whether accuracy and execution coverage survive stratification by collection policy. [Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems, NeurIPS 2015](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/5656-hidden-technical-debt-in-machine-learning-systems)
