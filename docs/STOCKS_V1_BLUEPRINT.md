# KESTREL Stocks v1.0 · A research desk that can change its mind

Scope covers Stocks research and simulated PAPER on one Dell. This blueprint records the product design. The handoff covers the shipped workflow, how to start the comparisons and which measurements still need operator data. [Build and operator handoff](STOCKS_V1_HANDOFF.md) · [Research papers and methods](STOCKS_V1_RESEARCH.md)

## What you can use now

- In **Learning**, select either small account and open its stored PAPER decision. Order rows link to the same records. Decision replay reveals the reason and later outcomes after you choose what PAPER did. The transfer review compares recorded research estimates with closed, fee-inclusive results where the exit policies align.
- In **Plays**, shortlist options and compare holding cash with the one-unit expiry scenarios. Recorded historical plays show displayed depth for synchronized receipts and the spread cost for the last supported exit at the selected size.
- In **Research**, review each book's latest decision and the current experiment coverage. Start or pause the preregistered IV study and the discretionary quote comparison independently. The quote comparison substitutes one eligible slot within the existing rotation budget and measures quote usability, operation counts, collection time and retained bytes.
- The first quote outcome is a measure of chain coverage. Separately testing whether a new quote changes the cash decision or improves later execution is an extension. True broker fills, untouched forward effectiveness, resource costs and an operator usability comparison require observation on the Dell.

## The product

Give KESTREL a research budget as well as a PAPER balance. Each morning it should be able to answer “What is worth finding out today?” Each evening it should show which decisions changed, which attractive ideas failed, and which observations justified the effort. A user should be able to open yesterday's decision and see precisely what the desk could have known, even after today's model or provider data changes.

The organizing object is a **decision record**. It describes the choice available to a specific book at a specific time and the single unresolved fact most likely to change that choice. Start with explicit, reproducible rules for the reasoning. A language model can later explain a stored record in plain language, with links to its inputs. The laptop must be able to collect, decide, abstain and recover offline from any text-generation service.

Imagine opening the app and reading the following **invented example**:

> A bullish spread leads the model. One unit requires $181.30 including entry fees. The $1K book has $70 available. The $5K book has enough cash, but its last receipt has no size on the leg it must sell. PAPER is waiting. A fresh receipt with both legs and sufficient size would settle the quote question. Your cash benchmark stays visible.

An hour later the app should be able to say “Both legs now have size; the spread widened and the order no longer meets the limit.” That is a successful investigation. The system found a reason to decline the trade. Paying for more predictions would have been less informative than collecting that quote.

## How the research loop works

1. **Observe.** Keep provider, instrument, venue/feed scope when supplied, market event time, receipt time, session and revision. Preserve raw references and transform versions. Classify delayed, absent, crossed, zero-size and stale observations. Normalize clocks in UTC and apply the exchange calendar for decisions. A late correction creates a later record.
2. **Form a claim and a competing explanation.** Example: expensive calls relative to puts may contain information about the stock. Dividends, difficult borrow, illiquidity and skewed coverage can also produce that observation. Freeze the feature, universe, horizon, comparator, minimum economic effect, stopping rule, and allowed data versions before enrolling a forward case.
3. **Choose a feasible action for each book.** Evaluate the leading candidate, a simple risk/holding-period-matched alternative, and holding cash. Use the book's available buying power and the existing cash-reservation policy. Show target allocation separately from feasibility. Track the stages candidate → risk-qualified → affordable → ordered → partially/fully filled → closed → graded. Keep no-op decisions and missing denominators.
4. **Find the decision boundary.** Ask what observable would change the result today. For a debit spread use the stored maximum acceptable debit, whole-unit capacity, and available cash. For a data-poor stock signal use the minimum pair count or age allowed by its protocol. State whether a new observation could plausibly change the decision before expiry. Refreshing quotes for a permanently unaffordable contract seldom helps that book.
5. **Spend a bounded data request where it can resolve a question.** Compare the decision before and after receipt. Keep unchanged, worse, invalidated, expired and unreachable cases. Ground the first prioritizer in explicit eligibility and a deterministic tie-break. Earn a learned acquisition policy with a prospective comparison.
6. **Grade distinct questions.** A directional stock forecast, a spread's expiry payoff, an executable early exit and a book's fee-inclusive cash outcome have different targets. Give every grade its instrument, clock, capital base, fees and comparator. Grade a marketability observation independently of what PAPER eventually did.
7. **Update policy only on new evidence.** Replay generates challengers. Untouched future decisions adjudicate frozen challengers. Promote, retain, restrict to a tested regime, or retire. Record which evidence would reverse the verdict. The prior model remains usable by ID for exact reconstruction.

### The smallest durable record

Use the existing SQLite databases, frozen play IDs, specs and ledger IDs. Bind records by identity and time. A symbol can have multiple strategies, expirations and accounts on the same day. Keep the event sequence append-only. Large chains and model files stay in their current stores and keep their retention budgets. If a reference may be pruned, freeze the small set of input values and flags required to rederive that exact decision.

| Event | Fields the next engineer must be able to recover |
| --- | --- |
| Decision frozen | Protocol hash and inception, book or study, exchange session, cutoff, candidate and comparator identities, cash and reserves at cutoff, units, model version, input values/references, observation and receipt times, exclusion reasons. |
| Observation scheduled | Question, candidate pool eligibility, intervention and comparator allocation, prior state, observed threshold, priority reason, deadline, budget unit, unique attempt ID. |
| Receipt or terminal failure | Provider and clock, request count when instrumented, elapsed time, payload bytes, contract identities/sides/size, quality flags, resulting state, and reason for missing coverage. |
| Outcome and verdict | Planned horizon, actual exit or expiry, exact ledger references, prices/fees/units, evidence class, baseline outcome, unresolved and rejected cases. Revisions point to the original. |

Use a uniqueness constraint on protocol, decision session, book/study and case identity. Write intent before optional network work. Reconcile interruption after a receipt from stored evidence. If the process dies after a provider request but before receipt persistence, record delivery as unknown and apply the retry budget. SQLite can prevent a duplicate decision or ledger effect. An unaffiliated market-data API cannot guarantee exactly-once network delivery.

### A first scientific experiment with a clear losing outcome

The repository already computes a matched-strike call-minus-put IV spread and has a historical weekly replay. Extend it into a prospective test of a **stock selection** decision. Paper trades in options are a later transfer experiment.

- Before next open, freeze the price-only momentum leader and a peer in the same sector within fixed momentum and volatility tolerances. Predeclare the universe, liquid-pair requirement and deterministic tie-break. The challenger chooses on the open-interest-weighted call-minus-put IV spread. The two arms share the price feature eligibility and fixed holding period. Require the coverage of both to be visible. Compare SPY over exactly the same interval and record beta, dividends, earnings, borrow availability and data gaps as available.
- Use next open through the fifth close for both arms. Label unadjusted stock **price return** explicitly. Resolve corporate actions with known-at-the-time receipts or mark the pair inconclusive. A dollar return per account or an option-fill result is a separate experiment.
- The draft protocol sets a one-volatility-point feature difference, a ten-basis-point economic hurdle, 30 prescheduled five-session blocks and at least 24 graded disagreements. These are design choices to review before enrollment. Simulate power under credible dispersion and missingness; if underpowered, extend the preregistered horizon **before** the first live case. Show exploratory overlapping days separately. Nonoverlap reduces mechanical dependence; test sensitivity to serial dependence and shared names. Keep a trial register and allow one primary test per version. Treat a sign test on its assumed units as conditional evidence.
- Run placebo and ablation challengers in historical replay with identical eligibility. Remove options, momentum, fundamentals, events and learned rankings in turn wherever present. Shuffle within date and comparable liquidity buckets. Purge train/test overlaps for the full holding window. Report differences per intent with uncertainty and sample loss. A positive placebo or gains confined to economically unfillable names blocks escalation.
- Ship a result that can say “the simple rule won,” “too few comparable opportunities,” or “direction improved but trading costs consumed the advantage.” Abstention rate, shared market exposure, regime mix and the loss from hard-to-observe cases belong next to returns.

### The data experiment that can pay for itself

Start with recorded Schwab/Tradier chains and the sources already present. A post-close chain snapshot can answer a cross-sectional question and still miss the intraday exit. SEC filings can establish disclosure dates; distinguish filing acceptance, event date and estimated earnings windows. Use Massive/reference and existing corporate-action data for endpoint verification. Never infer buyer-to-open direction or queue priority from aggregate volume.

For each eligible investigation, replay the current decision with the *range* of inputs allowed by its rule. Give precedence to questions that can actually change affordability, admissibility, or a queued limit within the decision horizon. While learning the policy, reserve random exploration among equally eligible cases. Allocate only discretionary capacity after held contracts, settlement and explicit operator requests. Preserve minimum rotation coverage. Record every eligible arm even if the provider times out. A day with no capacity should show why.

Evaluate the selection policy against ordinary rotation under the same budget using preassigned time blocks or paired slots with comparable liquidity and market hours. Record assignment probabilities, overlap and attrition. Use the observed fraction of resolved questions, subsequent useful decisions, disqualifications and coverage of under-sampled names. A policy that manufactures easy state changes while reducing eventual grading coverage loses. Provider cost can be $0 marginal under an existing subscription yet still consume rate quota, time and disk. Instrument request count, bytes and wall time separately.

**What “10×” would mean here.** If ordinary rotation needs 250 requests per complete, decision-changing record and the new policy needs 25, the ratio is 10. Those values are hypothetical. Freeze the definition of a useful record, randomize comparable collection slots, include failures, report counts and interval estimates, and repeat in another market regime. A tenfold speed or collection improvement says nothing on its own about return.

### Execution that respects what the tape contains

For a proposed close, align the actual legs by contract, signed ratio, receipt group, source and market timestamp. Sell held longs at a valid bid and buy back shorts at a valid ask. At each aligned receipt the displayed whole-unit upper bound is the minimum eligible side size divided by the absolute leg ratio, rounded down. Missing size is unknown. Displayed size can disappear before execution; multi-leg routing may behave differently. Separate simulated fills, displayed opportunities and unobservable queue/complex-book execution.

Show the midpoint, executable-side scenario, and the conservative size-constrained scenario on the same entry/exit clocks. Apply fees at the events when charged. Freeze planned limits and exits. Show partial fills, cancellations, official settlement, historical deliverable changes and missing evidence. Estimate adverse selection with a preregistered signed subsequent midpoint markout for the same contract at a fixed lag and tolerance, with its missing-pair denominator. A best tick selected after seeing the path belongs only in a clearly labelled diagnostic.

A common stress control on Plays lets a person compare whole-unit expiry payoffs with holding cash under the same percentage move. For different underlyings label this as an equal-percentage shock scenario. Do not silently equate it with a joint probability or a pre-expiry sale. Resolve the $1K and $5K book feasibility separately. A strategy with good mean return per filled trade may lose per submitted intent or be unusable for the smaller account.

## Learning from the plays it turns down

Keep a small prospectively sampled book of declined opportunities. Each sampled case records the actual reason for passing, the selection probability, the cheapest feasible alternative, and how it would be graded later. Follow it on the same clock and with the same price requirements as accepted ideas. An unaffordable play can supply a research observation about direction while contributing zero deployable opportunities for that book. Keep the two counts visible. Extend the existing rejected-idea sampling in `learner/forward.py` before creating another store.

Ask the desk to find its own recurring mistakes. Did a spread threshold reject many subsequently marketable, fee-positive trades? Was apparent success explained by rejected losers it never followed? Have the system nominate one narrow change, freeze it, and race it against the unchanged policy on future matched cases. Publish rejected winners, avoided losses, unobservable exits and the opportunity cost of waiting. Grade the new rule prospectively.

Build a **blind replay**. Show a real archived decision with only the information available then. Let the operator predict what KESTREL did, identify its weakest input, or choose cash. Reveal the next receipt and eventual outcome one step at a time. An operator can practice reading the system, and the team can discover which labels cause predictable mistakes. Reuse the historical/replay routes and keep user choices separate from study labels. Measure whether practice improves later decisions on different cases.

## The screen an operator should want to open

Home should answer one question first, in the operator's own book. Examples include “Why is PAPER waiting?”, “What changed since I last looked?” and “What evidence would make this play affordable and tradable?” Link directly to the decision, not just the top of a tab. Preserve account, case, scroll position and as-of time through refresh, Back and a phone rotation. Keep the full specialist views as drill-downs.

In the decision view, show **Observed** with a source/time, **Estimated** with a target/horizon and sample count, and **Missing** with a consequence and the next scheduled event. Place cash, reserved funds if authoritative, amount required for a whole unit and maximum modeled loss together. Show a reference price with its age. Put the simplest alternative and the cash outcome beside the leader. An operator can expand “What would change this?” to see a concrete threshold and the actual feed that could resolve it. A phone screen opens on the decision and the reason; charts and technical detail follow.

Test four tasks in counterbalanced order with representative users on desktop and phone: locate a viable idea, identify which book can afford one unit, explain why a wait is justified, and distinguish the model's expiry estimate from an observed exit. Record completion without assistance, median time, wrong-book errors, evidence-class errors and failures to return to the original idea. Pilot proposed gates of 90% correct book/evidence classification, zero cases where missing quotes are interpreted as verified fills, and at least 30% lower median task time with no drop in completion. Publish small sample counts and failures. Rendering tests and human performance answer different questions.

## Operation, red-team checks and subtraction

- **Reliability engineering challenges finance.** Kill after reservation, receipt insert, ledger commit and before report publish. Inject expired provider sessions, disk-full writes and repeated migration starts against disposable databases. Verify ledger totals, unique decision keys, report recovery, explicit progress/last successful receipt and bounded retry. Make retries idempotent and keep timeouts for optional research outside PAPER settlement.
- **Microstructure challenges ML.** Withhold one leg, provide size on the wrong side, stale times, mismatched receipt groups and a partial fill across sessions. The system should still grade the stock hypothesis when its own endpoints exist. It should abstain on the execution claim when its evidence is absent.
- **Asset pricing challenges acquisition.** Repeat evaluation on names the collector rarely selects. Attribute differences to coverage, volatility, sector and market exposure. Maintain a random comparison sample and a record of which names were never observable.
- **HCI challenges confidence.** Ask someone to explain why an account with positive equity cannot buy an idea, or why both books can be idle after using their deposits. If they need to infer the answer from multiple charts, fix the display before adding another score.
- **ML challenges the strategy catalogue.** Challenge committee votes, multiple highly correlated chance outputs, duplicate category rankings and weekly refits with the existing learner-ablate workflow. Run with a frozen incumbent and compute time to train, peak RSS and supported forward cases. Collapse a feature behind detail or retire its computation if it cannot beat a simpler alternative on the same decisions and exposure. Keep historical versions resolvable.
- Measure cold and warm startup, time to first populated view, collection lag against intended session, p95 PAPER commit delay, peak RSS, GB/week, provider quota use and weekly fit minutes on the Dell. Compare equivalent source/session states. A 7-day soak should produce zero duplicated paper ledger effects and a visible explanation for every missed session. Keep latency and memory targets provisional until operator measurements exist.
